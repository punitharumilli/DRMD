Digital Reference Material Document (DRMD)

The DRMD is an XML format for the documents that accompany a reference material: certificates and product information sheets. Its content follows ISO 33401:2024, so that certified values, uncertainties and statements can be read and checked by software.

The DRMD is developed at the Bundesanstalt für Materialforschung und -prüfung (BAM). The schema, the guidance document and the generator are openly available for everyone to use.

What a DRMD contains

A DRMD is a single XML file with one root element. Everything ISO 33401:2024 asks a certificate or product information sheet to state has a fixed place in it, so software always finds it in the same place.

Two document types

Reference material certificate
Accompanies a certified reference material. At least one property value carries a measurement uncertainty, and the document states metrological traceability.
Product information sheet
Accompanies any other reference material. Uncertainty and traceability are permitted but not required.

Structure of a DRMD document

digitalReferenceMaterialDocument

  • administrativeDataTitle, document identifier, version, period of validity, the producer and the persons responsible.
  • materialsEach reference material with its name, description, minimum sample size and identifiers.
  • propertiesListProperty values with units in the D-SI format, their uncertainties and the measurement procedures.
  • statementsIntended use, commutability, storage, handling and use, metrological traceability, health and safety.
  • commentoptionalRemarks on the document.
  • documentoptionalAn embedded human-readable copy, such as the original PDF.
  • ds:SignatureoptionalXML digital signature.
