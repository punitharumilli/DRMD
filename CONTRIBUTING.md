# Contributing

This page is maintained by the Bundesanstalt für Materialforschung und -prüfung (BAM).

## Reporting an error or asking for a change

Please open an issue in this repository, or write to [drmd@bam.de](mailto:drmd@bam.de). Name the
section of the page and describe what is wrong or missing.

BAM reviews every request. Accepted changes are made by BAM in this repository and published on
the page.

## Pull requests

Pull requests are welcome; please open an issue first. Keep the page free of scripts and of files
from other servers: the security policy at the top of `index.html` blocks them. A change that adds,
updates or removes third-party material (a font, an image) also adds or updates its licence and
copyright notice in `README.md` and next to the files.

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to do so, and you agree that it
is published under the licence of the files it changes: CC BY 4.0 for text, the EUPL v1.2 for code
(see the section "Licence" in `README.md`; GitHub Terms of Service, section D.6).

## Changed copies

CC BY 4.0 allows you to share and adapt the text, and the EUPL v1.2 the code. If you do, you must
credit BAM, keep the licence notices and say that you changed the page, and you must not suggest
that BAM endorses your version. The licences do not cover the BAM logo, the DRMD logo or the icons
made from it: a changed copy must not carry them (see the [Logo Policy](LOGO_POLICY.md)).
